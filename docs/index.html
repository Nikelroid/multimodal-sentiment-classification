Deep Learning · Multimodal Fusion

Multimodal Sentiment Classification

One model, three senses. Text, image, and audio are encoded by RoBERTa, ViT, and wav2vec2, fused into a single representation, and classified into negative, neutral, or positive sentiment — trained on the MSCTD dialogue dataset.

View source on GitHub Try the playground

Architecture

Late fusion of frozen-vocabulary transformer encoders with a compact MLP head.

Text
RoBERTa-base
Pooled sentence embedding, 768-d
Vision
ViT-B/16
Scene embedding from the frame, 768-d
Audio
wav2vec2-base
Mean-pooled waveform features, optional
→
Fusion
Concat → MLP
Negative · Neutral · Positive
